What We Do

Our Property Inspection Services

Explore our professional inspection services designed to find possible issues, document defects clearly, and help protect your property investment in Hyderabad.

01

Home Inspection

A full room-by-room check of your property's visible structure, electrical, plumbing, and interior parts.

Key Benefits
  • Full property condition snapshot in one visit
  • Early detection of costly hidden defects
  • Actionable report with photos and severity ratings
02

Building Defect Report

A focused, detailed report covering visible cracks, construction defects, workmanship issues, and finishing concerns.

Key Benefits
  • Formal defect list ready to share with the builder
  • Photo evidence of every finding, location-tagged
  • Priority grouping (critical / major / minor)
03

Water Leak Detection

Trace hidden water leaks, dampness, and moisture buildup using moisture meters and thermal imaging.

Key Benefits
  • Pinpoints leak source without breaking walls
  • Prevents long-term structural damage and mould
  • Repair recommendations, not guesswork
04

Electrical Inspection

A visual safety and quality check of your home's electrical installation, making sure it is safe and meets code.

Key Benefits
  • Reduces risk of electrical fires and shocks
  • Confirms wiring meets safety standards
  • Identifies load-balance issues before overload
05

Plumbing Inspection

Detailed check of accessible plumbing systems to flag poor workmanship, low pressure, and moisture-prone areas.

Key Benefits
  • Detect leaks and drainage issues early
  • Verify fittings and fixtures are installed correctly
  • Prevents water damage to walls and floors
06

Roof Inspection

Full check of your roof structure, waterproofing, drainage, and visible signs of damage or wear.

Key Benefits
  • Identifies cracked or blocked drainage points
  • Checks waterproofing before monsoon season
  • Prevents leaks that damage ceilings and walls
07

Thermal Scanning

Infrared thermal imaging shows temperature changes that are invisible to the naked eye, including hidden dampness and hot spots.

Key Benefits
  • Finds hidden defects without demolition
  • Detects electrical hot spots before fires
  • Visual thermal evidence in your report
08

Maintenance Consultation

Expert advice on property upkeep, preventive maintenance schedules, and planning for future repairs.

Key Benefits
  • Yearly maintenance plan tailored to your home
  • Preventive schedule to avoid costly repairs
  • Budget planning support for future upkeep
