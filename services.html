What We Do

Our Services

Explore our professional inspection services designed to identify potential issues, document defects clearly, and help protect your property investment.

01

Home Inspection

A comprehensive, room-by-room assessment of your property's visible structural, electrical, plumbing, and interior components.

02

Building Defect Report

A focused, documentation-heavy report covering visible cracks, construction defects, workmanship issues, and finishing concerns.

03

Water Leak Detection

Trace hidden water leaks, dampness, and moisture accumulation using moisture meters and thermal imaging.

04

Electrical Inspection

A visual safety and quality assessment of your home's electrical installation, ensuring safety and code compliance.

05

Plumbing Inspection

Detailed inspection of accessible plumbing systems to flag poor workmanship, low pressure, and moisture-prone areas.

06

Roof Inspection

Comprehensive check of your roof structure, waterproofing, drainage, and visible signs of damage or wear.

07

Thermal Scanning

Infrared thermal imaging reveals temperature anomalies invisible to the naked eye, including hidden dampness and hot spots.

08

Maintenance Consultation

Expert advice on property upkeep, preventive maintenance schedules, and planning for future repairs.
