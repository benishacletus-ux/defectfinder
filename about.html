Our Origin Story

Why DefectFinder was started.

DefectFinder started because we kept seeing the same problem in Hyderabad's property market. Families and NRI investors bought homes they thought were ready. Then, after the builder's handover form was signed, they found leaking bathrooms, cracked walls, faulty electricals, and unfinished work.

We saw that the real problem was not a lack of contractors or engineers. The real problem was the lack of an independent voice. Someone whose only job was to look at the property honestly and tell the owner the truth, before the money changed hands.

That is the gap we set out to fill. From day one, DefectFinder has worked as a third-party, unaffiliated property inspection service in Hyderabad. We are paid by the client, never by the builder, contractor, or seller. This independence is the base of everything we do.

Who We Are

A trusted independent partner for property owners.

DefectFinder.Pvt.Ltd is an independent property inspection, home renovation, and technical guidance company based in Hyderabad, Telangana. We serve two main groups: NRI property owners who need eyes and expertise on the ground while they are abroad, and local homeowners, buyers, and investors who want to be sure the property they are paying for is actually what they expect.

Our team includes civil engineers, structural specialists, electrical and plumbing technicians, and interior quality auditors. All of them are trained to look at a property the way a careful buyer would, but with the tools and expertise to find things a casual walkthrough would miss. We do not sell construction materials, we do not accept commissions from builders, and we do not offer "favourable" reports in exchange for repeat work.

What we do offer is a systematic inspection, a photo-rich digital report within 24–48 hours, and a candid conversation about what we found and what it means for you. From new flat handover inspections and pre-purchase checks to renovation planning and post-construction quality checks, DefectFinder is the technical partner you can trust when the stakes are highest.

Our Expertise

Deep technical skill across the built environment.

Every inspection is done by specialists who understand what they are looking at — not generalists ticking boxes.

Civil & Structural Check

Cracks, settlement, plaster quality, RCC condition, waterproofing integrity, and structural warning signs found by qualified civil engineers.

Plumbing & Waterproofing

Hidden leaks, seepage, damp patches, pipe joint failures, and terrace or bathroom waterproofing issues traced with moisture meters and thermal imaging.

Electrical & MEP Systems

Wiring standards, DB load balancing, earthing, switch and socket quality, and HVAC readiness checked against Indian safety norms.

Interior & Finishing Quality

Tile alignment, joinery, paint finish, modular fittings, false ceiling integrity, and premium-finish checks against the promised specification.

Thermal & Diagnostic Imaging

Infrared thermal scans and non-invasive diagnostic tools that reveal defects invisible to the naked eye — dampness, insulation gaps, and hidden hot spots.

Reporting & Documentation

Clear, photo-rich PDF reports with defect severity ratings, location tags, and actionable recommendations your builder or contractor can act on.

Our Professional Approach

How we work, from first call to final report.

A disciplined, repeatable process that makes sure every property gets the same level of care and every client gets the same level of clarity.

01

Consultation & Scope Definition

We start with a short call to understand your property type, your concerns, and your timeline. Based on this, we suggest the right inspection package and confirm the scope in writing — no surprise add-ons later.

02

On-Site Systematic Inspection

Our engineers arrive with a structured checklist, calibration tools, and diagnostic equipment. We inspect every accessible part — from the terrace to the drainage to the electrical panel — and photograph every finding at the moment it's observed.

03

Independent, Unbiased Analysis

Findings are analysed against Indian building standards and the property's promised specification. We do not soften or exaggerate results — defects are grouped by severity (Critical / Major / Minor) and documented factually.

04

Report Delivery & Walkthrough

You receive a full PDF report within 24–48 hours, complete with photos, location tags, severity ratings, and suggested remedies. We then walk you through the key findings personally, in language you can act on.

05

Follow-Up & Resolution Support

Our work does not end with the report. We stay available for follow-up questions, help you share findings with your builder or contractor, and can quote for repairs or renovation if you'd like us to fix what we found.
