Frequently Asked Questions

What does a property inspection cover?
Our inspection covers all major visible defects, including civil finish, plumbing, electricals, dampness, structural cracks, and tile alignment. We make sure your home meets the required quality standards before you move in.
How long does an inspection take?
A standard inspection for a 2 or 3 BHK flat takes about 2 to 4 hours. Larger homes or villas may take a little longer, as we check every corner of the property.
When will I get my inspection report?
You will get a full PDF report with high-resolution photos and technical recommendations within 48 hours of the inspection.
Do I need to be present during the inspection?
It is up to you. You are welcome to join us so we can show you issues in person, but it is not required. Many of our NRI clients are not present, and our detailed report tells them everything they need to know.
Do you also fix the defects you find?
Yes. We also offer home renovation and interior modification services. We can give you a quote to fix any major issues or upgrade your space after the inspection.
